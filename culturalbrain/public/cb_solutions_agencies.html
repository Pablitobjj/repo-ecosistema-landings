01

Keep client knowledge

Connect a brand's briefs, sources and assumptions to Brand Knowledge so the next pitch or campaign does not restart from zero.Conectá briefs, fuentes y supuestos con Brand Knowledge para que cada pitch o campaña no empiece desde cero.

02

Define and research audiences

Audience Builder, TAR and the seven Research Center studies help distinguish client-defined targets, suggested profiles and cultural evidence.Audience Builder, TAR y los siete estudios de Research Center ayudan a separar targets declarados, perfiles propuestos y evidencia cultural.

03

Build evidence-informed media lists

Site Analysis, Media Discovery, SitePlanner and SiteList Manager compare environments and curate candidates using CRS, CAI and CCS with proper context.Site Analysis, Media Discovery, SitePlanner y SiteList Manager comparan entornos y curan candidatos con CRS, CAI y CCS interpretados en contexto.

04

Reason with CEREBRO; work with Sofía

CEREBRO tests explanations and exposes gaps. Sofía is the account-facing Strategic Account Manager who helps your team navigate insights and supported workflows.CEREBRO contrasta explicaciones y expone vacíos. Sofía es la Strategic Account Manager que acompaña al equipo entre hallazgos y flujos de trabajo.

05

Plan and hand off

Bring findings to Media Planner, campaign and IO workflows; prepare activation briefs and files for the operators—without claiming automatic DSP media buying.Llevá los hallazgos a Media Planner, campañas e IO; prepará briefs y archivos de activación para operadores sin prometer compra automática en DSP.

06

Explain what changed

Review campaign results in Campaign Monitor, document hypotheses and verdicts in Learning Loop, and use Pulse to revisit changing or uncovered evidence.Revisá resultados con Campaign Monitor, documentá hipótesis y veredictos en Learning Loop y usá Pulse para revisar evidencia cambiante o no cubierta.
