Our methodologyNuestra metodología

Six steps to make cultural evidence useful.Seis pasos para convertir evidencia cultural en decisiones útiles.

Hispaño investigates cultural meaning in relation to a concrete marketing problem. The method is a decision framework, not a claim that every source or sample applies everywhere.Hispaño investiga el significado cultural en relación con un problema concreto de marketing. El método es un marco de decisiones, no la promesa de que todas las fuentes o muestras sirven para cualquier mercado.

01

Start with the decision

Empezar por la decisiónDefine the brand or media problem, audience universe, market, period and a testable question.

02

Understand cultural context

Comprender el contexto culturalTreat identity, language, generation, place and category as intersecting research variables—not fixed consumer types.

03

Choose the evidence

Seleccionar evidenciaSeparate brand-provided data, observed external sources, modelled signals and synthetic examples; document provenance.

04

Read media environments

Analizar entornos de mediosInvestigate the context and suitability of sites and media; consider what cultural relevance, affinity or cohesion can and cannot measure.

05

Compare explanations

Contrastar explicacionesWrite alternative cultural hypotheses, limitations and what evidence would change the interpretation.

06

Translate into action

Convertir en accionesConnect research with audience, creative and media decisions; define a measurable check and the next review.

Research rigorRigor de investigación

The source, the interpretation and the recommendation are different things.La fuente, la interpretación y la recomendación son cosas distintas.

A useful cultural intelligence output makes its universe and limits visible. It does not treat correlations or scores as proof of individual behavior or campaign effectiveness.Un hallazgo útil identifica universo y límites. No convierte correlaciones ni scores en prueba de conducta individual o efectividad publicitaria.

ObservedObservado

A documented signal from a defined source, period and geographic coverage.Señal documentada de una fuente, período y geografía definidos.

Estimated or modelledEstimado o modelado

A derived profile, proxy or score with a stated method, scale and uncertainty.Perfil, proxy o indicador derivado, con método, escala e incertidumbre explícitos.

InterpretationInterpretación

A reasoned hypothesis about the cultural context, tested against plausible alternatives.Hipótesis razonada sobre el contexto cultural, contrastada con alternativas posibles.

Recommended decisionDecisión recomendada

A proposed action, owner, supporting evidence and measurable validation path.Acción propuesta, responsable, evidencia de respaldo y forma medible de validación.

01

Site Analysis & contextual suitability

Analyse a site's language, content and context; suitability is a contextual input, not a guarantee of fraud prevention.Analizar idioma, contenido y contexto de sitios; suitability es una señal contextual, no garantía antifraude.

02

CRS · cultural relevance

An indicator intended to contextualize cultural relevance. Read it only with its method, unit, version and evaluated universe.Indicador pensado para contextualizar relevancia cultural. Interpretarlo con método, unidad, versión y universo evaluado.

03

CAI · cultural affinity

An affinity signal for defined audiences and contexts, not a count of actual people or individual identity.Señal de afinidad para audiencias y contextos definidos, no un conteo de personas ni una identidad individual.

04

CCS · cultural cohesion

A cohesion-related indicator read within its specific model; not a universal, longitudinally certified metric.Indicador relacionado con cohesión dentro de un modelo específico, no una métrica longitudinal universal certificada.

05

Audience Builder & TAR

Distinguish who the brand defines, which profiles are suggested and which cultural or geographic signals support the analysis.Distinguir la audiencia definida por la marca, los perfiles propuestos y las señales culturales o geográficas que los sustentan.
